Yelahanka, located in North Bengaluru, has emerged as one of Karnataka's fastest-growing education corridors. With rapid residential expansion in Yelahanka New Town, Kogilu, Doddaballapura Road, and areas near Kempegowda International Airport, thousands of families now call North Bangalore home — and their children need quality Pre-University education close by.

This guide lists the top PU colleges in Yelahanka for 2026-27 admissions, with an honest comparison across key parameters: pass rate, streams, coaching, fees, and location.

Why Yelahanka is a Top Education Hub in North Bengaluru

According to the Department of Pre-University Education, Karnataka, Bengaluru Urban district has the highest concentration of PU colleges in the state — and Yelahanka accounts for a significant share of North Bengaluru's institutions.

  • 20+ recognised PU colleges within a 5 km radius of Yelahanka
  • Strong transport connectivity via BMTC buses, Namma Metro (Yellow Line), and NH 44
  • Proximity to Kempegowda International Airport — making it accessible from Devanahalli, Doddaballapura, and Bagalur
  • Growing residential population driving demand for quality local education
  • Several colleges offering integrated KCET, NEET, and JEE coaching

How to Choose the Right PU College in Yelahanka

With so many options, narrowing down the best PU college requires evaluating five key factors:

  1. Pass Rate — Aim for colleges with a consistent 95%+ pass rate in Karnataka PUC board exams.
  2. Stream & Combination — Confirm your preferred stream (PCMB, PCMC, CEBA, HEBA) is offered.
  3. Entrance Exam Coaching — For Science students, integrated NEET/KCET/JEE coaching saves money and commute time.
  4. Faculty Quality — Ask about teacher qualifications and years of experience.
  5. Location — A college within 3–5 km of home significantly reduces fatigue for students.

What is the fee structure for PU colleges in Yelahanka?

PU college fees in Yelahanka typically range from ₹20,000 to ₹80,000 per year depending on streams and facilities. Nagachethana PU College offers competitive fees. Contact +91 97390 85747 for current fee details.

When do PU college admissions start in Yelahanka for 2026-27?

PU college admissions in Yelahanka for 2026-27 typically open in March–April, after SSLC (10th) results are declared in May. Early applications are accepted from March. Nagachethana PU College is currently accepting enquiries for 2026-27.

Do PU colleges in Yelahanka offer hostel facilities?

Some PU colleges in Yelahanka (especially those affiliated with university groups like REVA) offer hostel facilities for outstation students. Standalone colleges like Nagachethana, NSAM, and Presidency typically do not, but many students find affordable PG accommodations near the college. It's best to contact the college directly about housing options.

What is the average fee for PCMB with coaching in Yelahanka PU colleges?

PCMB with integrated NEET/KCET coaching typically costs ₹35,000–₹60,000 per year at reputed colleges in Yelahanka. Colleges like Nagachethana PU College and NSAM PU College offer integrated coaching, which saves ₹50,000–₹1,00,000 annually compared to separate coaching institutes.

How many students typically score above 95% in PU exams from Yelahanka colleges?

Top-rated colleges in Yelahanka like Nagachethana, NSAM, and Presidency produce 10–20 students scoring 95%+ marks annually. Nagachethana PU College has produced 50+ toppers since 2015. These figures vary by college — ask for the last 3 years' merit lists when visiting.

Can I change streams after admission in Yelahanka PU colleges?

Stream changes in the first 2-3 weeks are typically allowed at most PU colleges in Yelahanka if seats are available. After that, Karnataka PUC board rules restrict changes. It's crucial to choose your stream wisely before joining. Most colleges offer stream counselling during admissions.

Which PU college in Yelahanka has the best sports facilities?

Nagachethana and Presidency colleges have decent facilities within their premises. For serious athletes, visiting the campus and checking courts/grounds in person is recommended.
